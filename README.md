# Open Inquiry Initiative — Public Preview v0.1

Open Inquiry Initiative is an evolving research program investigating human–computational understanding, spatial computing, artificial intelligence, simulations, and systems engineering. The initiative shares research questions, methods, prototypes, limitations, and reproducible evidence where available.

## Start here

- [Public project map](PROJECTS.md) for the projects and research directions in this preview.
- [Research status and evidence policy](RESEARCH_STATUS.md) for how we classify claims.
- [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) for corrections, reproduction reports, and questions.

## Research directions

- **Reality OS:** a proposed spatial computing environment with persistent worlds, recursive world creation, tool interfaces, and model interoperability. Implementation maturity must be verified per artifact before any claims are made.
- **Human–AI mutual grounding:** investigating whether humans and AI systems can build, repair, and sustain sufficient shared understanding to accomplish tasks.
- **Research cartography:** provenance-aware mapping of projects, assumptions, experiments, and uncertainty.
- **Systems integration:** conceptual and experimental exploration of distributed infrastructure, energy, thermal systems, and coupled planning.

## Principles

1. Publish uncertainty, negative findings, and limitations.
2. Preserve provenance and distinguish ideas from evidence.
3. Respect privacy, third-party rights, licenses, and security.
4. Invite reproducibility, criticism, and collaboration.

## How to participate

Review public repositories and open focused issues with reproducible observations, relevant references, corrections, or questions.
