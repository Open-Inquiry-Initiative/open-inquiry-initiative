# OII-GEO-001 — Strategic Computational Infrastructure Governance

Date: 2026-11-24 (US Central)  
Status: Preliminary research; public working research; ongoing public documentation authorized 2026-11-24.  
Method: Recursive Ascent and Branch Evaluation (RABE). AI-assisted documentation.

## Research question
Is US industrial policy expanding beyond regulation and subsidies into ownership and influence over leadership of strategically important computational infrastructure? The initiating observation was a social media headline about presidential pardon and Nvidia equity remarks; the headline is a lead, not evidence.

## Evidence ledger
- E1: Nvidia's Form 8-K reports a board decision reinstating Jensen Huang effective November 22, announced November 23. [Corporate filing](https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0001045810/d56080ad-0d8b-401a-b284-e3308341659a.pdf).
- E2: Reuters reports presidential advocacy for reinstatement; Huang reportedly disputes that pressure caused the decision. Distinguish advocacy from established causation. [Reuters, November 24](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/nvidia-slips-investors-assess-risks-surrounding-huangs-return-ceo-2026-11-24/).
- E3: Trump discussed possible clemency and a potential federal equity stake, not an executed transaction or pardon. [Original interview reporting](https://nypost.com/2026/11/24/us-news/trump-leaves-door-open-to-jensen-huang-pardon-and-urges-him-not-to-plead-5th-to-congress-next-week/); [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/trump-us-equity-stake-nvidia-not-too-late-2026-11).
- E4: Intel's August 2025 agreement describes a $8.9 billion investment equivalent to 9.9% ownership, passive voting arrangements with limited exceptions, no board representation, and a conditional warrant. [SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086325000129/a08222025form8-kex991.htm).
- E5: Congressional concerns and ongoing inquiries are documented; accusations are not adjudicated findings. [November 24 congressional letter](https://democrats-energycommerce.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-energycommerce.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026.11.24-multi-committee-letter-to-president-trump-re-jensen-huang.pdf).
- E6: Reporting describes US Steel golden-share rights and critical-minerals investments as additional precedents; exact terms require primary-document review (see E3). MP Materials is a candidate case for that review.

## Dependency model
Critical materials → chip fabrication → accelerators → data centers/cloud → AI models and research runtimes → applications (including Reality OS). Upstream ownership, supply restrictions and governance rights may affect downstream availability. Equity alone does not imply operational control. Distinguish passive holdings, warrants, golden shares, board rights, export controls and subsidies.

## Engineering implications
Maintain portable compute backends, local execution, open and forkable core software, interchangeable models via Hydra, and rendering-independent persistent world state. These are resilience measures, not evidence of political motives. Test portability, offline operation, dependency substitution and state migration before claiming independence.

## Next research gate

Record dated ownership percentages, voting rights, warrants, board roles, legal authority and conditions. Compare against historical US industrial policy and non-equity alternatives. Monitor proposed versus executed transactions separately. Reassess hypotheses only on new evidence; preserve negative findings. No monitoring automation authorized by this document.

## Limitations and correction log
This is a bounded documentary review, not a causal study or comprehensive legal audit. Primary Nvidia filing distinguishes the November 22 effective decision from November 23 announcement; earlier conversational summary conflated these. The exact market rally percentage is omitted because reporting varies by observation time. Presidential advocacy is documented but its causal effect is contested. No independent verification of private conversations, exact critical-minerals terms, or a government Nvidia acquisition was completed. General ownership precedent does not establish an upward trend without a historical baseline. The social-media allegation is not treated as proof. Public updates are authorized for this investigation, subject to source verification, privacy, rights and evidence standards.
