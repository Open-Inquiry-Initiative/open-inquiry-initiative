# Track B Research Intake Registry

This registry assigns unique research record IDs. Before adding a record, check the latest committed registry and reserve the next unused sequence for the date. Concurrent submissions must resolve collisions before merge. Do not assign identifiers merely from chat messages without writing the record.

## Operating rules
- Use `OII-B-YYYYMMDD-NNN` with three-digit daily sequence numbers.
- Preserve separate identifiers for independent branches of one discussion.
- An early-stage README is a valid intake artifact; do not overstate maturity.
- Review privacy, licensing and safety before public publication.
- A public record does not imply that its hypotheses are validated.
- Cross-link Track A milestones upon explicit promotion.
