# Standing research authorization — 2026-11-27

The founder authorizes formal designation, research write-ups and GitHub publication for approved projects and subsequent proposals falling within an already authorized research umbrella, without repeated individual approval.

## Operating interpretation
- Identify the parent program, scope, hypothesis, prior art, baselines, evidence requirements and limitations.
- Prefer extensions to existing programs over unnecessary proliferation of designations.
- Proceed with bounded computational research where already authorized and resources permit.
- Public-by-default remains subject to existing privacy, rights, security, evidence and responsible-release gates. Preserve negative results and distinguish hypotheses from validated findings.
- This authorization does not override separate approvals for human or animal experimentation, clinical interventions, hazardous physical work, spending, production deployment, sensitive disclosure, destructive changes or materially new research umbrellas.
- Record decisions and evidence in checkpoints; escalate genuine scope ambiguity or consequential risks.

## Purpose
Reduce repetitive approval friction while maintaining human oversight, reproducibility and the civilian mission of expanding equitable opportunities for inquiry. Human-machine symbiosis research emphasizes restoration, augmentation, autonomy, consent and accessibility; speculative outcomes are not promises.

Authorization applies prospectively to in-scope proposals, not blanket authority over unrelated actions. Existing governance and release safeguards remain in force.
