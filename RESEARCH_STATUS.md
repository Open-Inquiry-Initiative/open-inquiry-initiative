# Research status and evidence policy

All projects must distinguish the following states. Status is per claim or artifact, not a global endorsement of a repository.

| State | Meaning | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Question, interpretation, or proposed mechanism | Scope, assumptions, related work, limitations |
| Prototype | Implemented artifact exists | Source revision, run instructions, known defects |
| Experiment | Defined comparison or observation | Protocol, environment, logs, raw outcomes, negative results |
| Validated (bounded) | Specific result independently checked or reproducible within stated limits | Reproduction instructions, independent check, boundaries |
| Unreviewed | Evidence insufficient to assign another status | Explicitly marked pending |

The terms *consciousness*, *intelligence*, *understanding*, and *mutual grounding* are not interchangeable. Human–AI mutual grounding is a research framing for establishing, checking, and repairing shared task context, not evidence that an AI has humanlike experience.

Benchmark performance alone does not prove a system can sustain context-grounded collaboration; conversely, usability alone does not establish broad intelligence. Claims must identify the exact measurement and test conditions.
