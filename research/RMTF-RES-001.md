# RMTF-RES-001 — Resonant Field and Spacetime Coupling

Date: 2026-11-25. Public exploratory research protocol; not a demonstrated physical portal. AI-assisted drafting.

## Question
Can controlled resonant fields produce measurable spacetime curvature beyond conventional gravitational predictions, or enable an addressable traversable connection? Fictional Stargates, portal guns and pocket laboratories motivate the architecture, not the evidence.

## Baseline
A linear damped driven oscillator has normalized amplitude A(r)=1/sqrt((1-r²)²+(r/Q)²), where r is driving frequency divided by natural frequency and Q is quality factor. At r=1, A=Q. Acoustic waves require a medium; electromagnetic waves propagate in vacuum. Resonance does not itself establish remote coupling or alter topology.

Einstein equations relate curvature to stress-energy: G_mu_nu + Lambda g_mu_nu = 8 pi G T_mu_nu/c^4. Added stored energy E has mass equivalent E/c². Using c=299792458 m/s, 1 J corresponds to 1.113e-17 kg, 1 kJ to 1.113e-14 kg, 1 MJ to 1.113e-11 kg, and 1 GJ to 1.113e-8 kg. These correct the mass-unit conversions in the initial conversational draft. A mass equivalent of 1 kg requires about 8.99e16 J. Geometry and stresses matter; mass equivalence alone is not a full curvature calculation.

## Recursive Hydra / RABE branches
- A: Selective matter excitation; quantify coupling, damping and energy conservation.
- B: Remote synchronization; compare conventional electromagnetic coupling with null controls; respect causal propagation.
- C: Stress-energy and spacetime geometry; derive standard GR baseline, then test any proposed deviation. Examine energy conditions, stability and boundary conditions before claiming traversability.
- D: Reality OS simulation: destination identifiers, coordinate transforms, endpoint synchronization, connection validation, traversal and persistent destination state. Virtual success is not physical evidence.
