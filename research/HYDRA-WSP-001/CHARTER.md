# HYDRA-WSP-001 — Weight-Space Programming and Neural Capability Engineering

Approved: 2026-11-28. Status: authorized research; G00 pending. No experimental results claimed.

## Objective
Evaluate whether direct parameter-space programming can construct, repair or specialize neural networks reproducibly, with measurable advantages over conventional training, adapters and fixed specialists. Integrate only independently qualified capabilities into Hydra; visualize evidence in Reality OS.

## Gates
- G00: Verify primary literature, licenses, model provenance, hardware requirements and reproduction feasibility. Candidate leads (unverified): https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/ and https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25476.
- G01: Interpret weights using blinded synthetic networks, unseen functions and permutation controls.
- G02: Construct small neural networks from formal specifications; compare outputs against executable references.
- G03: Test targeted parameter repairs against held-out tasks and unrelated capability retention.
- G04: Evaluate reusable capability installation and compositional interference.
- G05: Compare direct edits with LoRA, conventional training, unmodified models and matched random edits under matched resource budgets.
- G06: Evaluate Hydra-directed specialization in isolated candidate copies; no autonomous production modification.
- G07: Independent verification, regression and safety testing, immutable originals, signed approval and exact rollback.
- G08: Reality OS visualization of architecture, parameter diffs, uncertainty and evidence lineage.
- G09: Publish cleared reproducible code, manifests, negative results and independent reproduction status.

## Qualification protocol
Preregister seeds, splits, metrics and acceptance thresholds before experiments. Measure task accuracy, retention, edit sparsity, compute, memory, latency, reproducibility and failure rates. Separate exploratory results from held-out confirmation. Record confidence intervals and failed trials. Begin with CPU-sized synthetic networks; qualify RTX 2070 SUPER feasibility separately. Treat published performance claims as unverified until checked against primary evidence.

## Governance
Public-by-default subject to rights, privacy and safety review. Preserve immutable source checkpoints and hashes. Restrict edits to authorized local models; do not bypass safeguards or modify deployed systems. Candidate generation and acceptance must remain separate. No self-approval, recursive autonomous deployment or implied production authorization. Failed or ambiguous candidates remain quarantined. Independent reproduction and explicit deployment approval are separate gates.
