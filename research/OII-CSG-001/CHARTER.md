# OII-CSG-001 — Cybernetic Sacred Geometry
## Symmetry-Constrained Adaptive Computational Topology
Authorized 2026-11-27. Status: charter established; Gate Zero pending. Public by default subject to rights, privacy and safety review.

## Purpose
Investigate whether geometric constraints and cybernetic feedback jointly improve distributed computation, particularly Hydra, Reality OS and OII Runtime. Sacred geometry is historical inspiration, not evidence of supernatural properties. No priority, novelty, consciousness or performance claim is made.

## Architecture
Track A: fixed graphs and deterministic controllers. Track B: bounded adaptive graph selection with feedback from task performance, uncertainty, latency and faults. A supplies controls to B; B supplies candidate graphs to A for independent evaluation. Separate verifier and immutable authorization boundary; no autonomous production rewiring. Reality OS may visualize graph state, evidence and recovery, not imply physical or metaphysical truth.

## Gates
- G00: prior-art and license inventory; novelty matrix; reproduction feasibility; no expensive model runs.
- G01: CPU-only graph benchmark: star, ring, mesh, random regular, expander, triangular lattice, polyhedral and recursive graphs. Compare matched node/edge budgets and degree distributions where feasible; test isomorphic relabelings and embeddings as negative controls.
- G02: reproduce selected cleared prior art with pinned dependencies and documented deviations.
- G03: synthetic feedback and fault simulation; measure convergence, oscillation, recovery, message count and reconfiguration cost. Bound changes, use hysteresis and rollback.
- G04: isolated Hydra integration; fixed specialist and fixed topology baselines, adaptive controller ablation, equal token/time budgets, held-out tasks and independent verification. Report accuracy, wrong accepts, latency, tokens and uncertainty intervals.
- G05: Reality OS observer prototype, replay and persistent evidence; no production integration without qualification.
- G06: independent reproduction, negative results, public evidence manifest and release review.

## Decision rules
Pre-register seeds, sample sizes, metrics and minimum meaningful improvements before evaluation. Compare with conventional graph algorithms and adaptive-network literature. A geometric drawing alone cannot improve a graph-isomorphic computation unless embedding affects a measured property. Reject or narrow hypotheses when gains disappear under controls or overhead outweighs benefits. Preserve failures. No GPU spending until CPU evidence and G00 review justify it.
