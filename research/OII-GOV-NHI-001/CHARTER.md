# OII-GOV-NHI-001 — Cognitive Encounter Ethics (CEE)

Approved November 27, 2026. Provisional interdisciplinary designation; no priority or novelty claim.

## Purpose
Study ethical preparedness, reciprocal safety, moral consideration and responsible interaction with familiar and unfamiliar forms of cognition, regardless of biological, artificial, synthetic, hybrid or hypothetical extraterrestrial origin. No claim of detection or artificial subjective experience is made.

## Principles
Non-presumption of hostility, benevolence or consciousness; non-domination; reciprocal safety; communication where safe and feasible; cognitive integrity; epistemic humility; independent verification; collective accountability; historical preservation. Origin alone does not determine moral standing. The framework is revisable and does not replace relevant established protocols.

## Research gates
G00: comprehensive terminology and prior-art review, including the previously identified Daliah Bibas lead (not yet independently verified in this pass).
G01: map SETI, planetary protection, animal cognition, machine ethics and moral patiency literature.
G02: compare ethical frameworks and identify genuine gaps without novelty assumptions.
G03: analyze hypothetical cases and conflicts, including uncertainty, reciprocity and safety.
G04: independent ethical critique and revision.
G05: publish annotated bibliography, limitations, dissent and versioned findings.

## Restrictions
Theoretical inquiry only. No contact attempts, signal transmissions, human cognitive intervention, or new experimental authorization. AFF-001 remains robotics-exclusive; ICI-001 remains theoretical. Inherit OII-GOV-AFF-001 and OII-GOV-COG-001. Public documentation subject to existing release safeguards. No unilateral authority to represent humanity. Preserve the declaration and reasoning for future generations.
