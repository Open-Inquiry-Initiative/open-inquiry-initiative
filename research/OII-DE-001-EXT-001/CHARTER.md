# OII-DE-001-EXT-001 — Traversable Spacetime Topologies and Computational Wormhole Dynamics

Parent: OII-DE-001. Approved for public GitHub publication 2026-11-28. Status: charter only; all research gates pending.

## Objective
Investigate mathematical traversability, tidal constraints, energy requirements and stability of hypothetical wormhole geometries using reproducible computational methods. No claim of physical realization or human safety is made.

## Gates
- G00: Verify primary paper, bibliographic metadata, prior art, licenses and reproduction feasibility. Candidate DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/aebade (verify before reliance). Secondary lead: https://www.sciencealert.com/physicist-finds-a-mathematical-way-to-make-wormholes-safe-for-human-travel
- G01: Reproduce published metric and stress-energy solutions; check Einstein-equation residuals and units.
- G02: Integrate timelike and null geodesics; compare analytic limits and numerical convergence.
- G03: Compute orthonormal-frame curvature and tidal acceleration; distinguish proper and coordinate quantities.
- G04: Evaluate energy-condition violations, integrated energy requirements and assumptions; distinguish dark energy from hypothetical exotic matter.
- G05: Investigate perturbations and stability under explicit matter-model and boundary assumptions; static solutions do not establish stability.
- G06: Explore cosmological constant and dark-energy interactions with matched controls.
- G07: Hydra-assisted parameter exploration with deterministic baselines, held-out tests, independent verification and preserved failures.
- G08: Reality OS Observatory visualization driven by validated numerical outputs, with clear labels for assumptions and uncertainty; publish code, manifests, raw evidence and limitations subject to rights review.

## Governance
Computational/theoretical scope only. No physical construction or human testing authorized. Preregister tolerances, seeds and acceptance criteria before benchmark runs. Preserve negative results. Public-by-default subject to provenance, licensing and privacy review. Independent reproduction and formal gate acceptance are separate from execution. No gate is claimed passed by this charter.
