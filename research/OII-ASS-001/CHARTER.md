# Adaptive Human Biomechanical Assistance

Founder approved 2026-11-27. Public research charter; no clinical efficacy or novelty established.

Mission: reduce suffering and preserve autonomy, mobility and quality of life. Assistive technology should not compel productivity despite pain.

## Portfolio
- OII-ASS-001 Adaptive Spinal Suspension: investigate pneumatic cushioning alongside a clear spinal channel and external torso-to-pelvis load paths; distinguish pressure redistribution from actual unloading.
- OII-ASS-002 Dynamic Joint Unloading
- OII-ASS-003 Wearable Pressure Redistribution
- OII-ASS-004 Predictive Movement Assistance
- OII-ASS-005 Patient-Directed Digital Pain Mapping
- OII-ASS-006 Personalized Biomechanical Simulation
- OII-ASS-007 Assisted Mobility and Fatigue Reduction

## Gates
G00 prior art, patents, licensing and independent verification of preliminary literature leads (Orthotrac, AireLevate, pneumatic corsets, soft robotic orthoses). G01 component feasibility: pumps, chambers, valves, pressure/force sensors, IMUs, controllers, load frames, batteries, mechanical relief and quick release. G02 computational musculoskeletal and pneumatic models, including OpenSim candidates. G03 matched passive, active and hybrid comparisons. G04 hazard analysis and independent safety limits, including compression, traction, balance, skin pressure, respiration, load transfer, power loss and sensor faults. G05 Reality OS visualization and virtual test integration. G06 non-human bench qualification and independent clinical and engineering review before any human testing.

## Evidence and safeguards
Initial scope computational and non-human bench research only. No improvised human trials, forced alignment, clinical claims or prescribed settings. Human research requires separate authorization, appropriate ethics/regulatory review and informed consent. Patient data private by default; public artifacts sanitized. Measure mechanical load, interface pressure, range of motion, stability, failure behavior and uncertainty; future patient-centered outcomes require validated clinical protocols. Preserve negative results, baselines, reproducible code and limitations. Hardware availability, clinical benefit and novelty remain unverified. No personal medical details are included.
