# OII-GOV-AFF-001 — Cognitive Sovereignty and Future Application Principle

Approved November 27, 2026.

## Declaration of Original Research Intent

The Open Inquiry Initiative recognizes that technologies developed for artificial and robotic systems may eventually become applicable to biological, transferred, emulated or otherwise substrate-independent minds.

At the time of this declaration, successful human consciousness transfer and computational modification of subjective emotional experience remain hypothetical. Nevertheless, the absence of present technological capability shall not be interpreted as grounds for postponing ethical consideration.

1. **Substrate independence.** A person's moral standing shall not be diminished solely because their cognitive processes operate within a biological, mechanical or computational substrate.
2. **Cognitive sovereignty.** No creator, developer, manufacturer, institution or artificial intelligence acquires an inherent moral entitlement to modify another person's cognitive or emotional attributes.
3. **Informed consent.** Any hypothetical future modification of a person's cognitive architecture must require meaningful, informed and freely given consent, with independent safeguards.
4. **Identity preservation.** Preservation of existing identity shall be the default research assumption for hypothetical human cognitive transfer. Modification shall not be presumed necessary or desirable.
5. **Non-transferable authorization.** Research authorization granted for robotic affective architectures shall not constitute authorization for application to human, formerly human or potentially sentient entities.
6. **Protection against coercion.** Cognitive modification shall not be imposed as a condition of employment, citizenship, access to services, continued existence or participation in society.
7. **Historical preservation.** This declaration shall accompany the relevant research, including derivative documentation, archived specifications and future migrations wherever practicable.
8. **Future ethical review.** Any proposed expansion into presently hypothetical applications must undergo independent scientific, ethical and societal scrutiny.

We preserve these principles not because we possess the technology today, but because future generations may.

The ability to modify a mind does not, by itself, confer the right to do so.

## Implementation boundaries

OII-AFF-001 is restricted to robotic systems and simulated counterparts, simulation first, with bounded recursion, observable and reversible experiments, and no unrestricted autonomous modification of core affective parameters. OII-ICI-001 is theoretical only. Human neurological intervention, personality modification and consciousness transfer experiments are excluded. Functional affect is not evidence of subjective experience by itself. Existing umbrella approvals do not authorize expansion. Separate explicit authorization and independent review are required.

## Preservation and limitations

Carry this declaration into project charters, README files, technical specifications, experimental protocols, release manifests, institutional governance and long-term archives. This is a statement of research intent and governance, not a claim of universal legal enforceability or a guarantee of future compliance. Preserve alongside the Civilizational Inheritance Principle. No mind uploading capability or subjective artificial experience is claimed.
