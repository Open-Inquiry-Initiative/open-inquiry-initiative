# OII-ORG-001 — Organoid Systems and Computational Biology

Authorized 2026-11-28. Status: charter; research results pending.

## Mission
Investigate organoid biology and information processing using public, rights-cleared data and computational models. No claim of cognition, consciousness, clinical efficacy or biological computing advantage is presumed.

## Branches
- ORG-001: Computational organoid modeling.
- ORG-002: Biological neural information processing (analysis of existing recordings).
- ORG-003: Simulated hybrid biological-digital interfaces.
- ORG-004: Cellular organization and emergent topology.
- ORG-005: Regenerative tissue modeling.
- ORG-006: Reality OS biological digital twins.
- ORG-007: Biological cognition ethics and safeguards.

## Qualification gates
G00: Prior art, repository overlap, dataset provenance, consent, licensing and ethics review. G01: Reproduce a published computational baseline with pinned dependencies and held-out evaluation. G02: Compare Hydra-assisted analysis with conventional baselines, including uncertainty and negative results. G03: Visualize validated model outputs in Reality OS with explicit simulated/observed labels. G04: Independent reproduction and public evidence review.

## Boundaries
Initial scope is computational and non-interventional. No specimen acquisition, culture, stimulation, transplantation or physical interface experiments are authorized by this charter. Any physical work requires separate institutional, ethical and biosafety review. Robotics authorization is not transferable to living tissue. Neural activity, learning, cognition and consciousness are distinct claims requiring distinct evidence. Protect donor privacy; do not publish restricted data. No clinical or therapeutic claims without appropriate validation.

## Evidence and publication
Public by default subject to rights, privacy and safety review. Preserve source provenance, code, parameters, seeds, raw permissible results, failures and limitations. Independent reproduction is required before qualification claims. No experiment or prior-art audit is represented as completed by this charter.
