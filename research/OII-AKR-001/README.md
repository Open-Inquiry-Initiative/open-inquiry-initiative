# OII-AKR-001 — Ancient Knowledge Reexamination

Authorized 2026-11-25 for public research. Status: charter and preliminary hypotheses only; no validated findings.

## Objective
Investigate historical writings using historical, linguistic, philosophical, mathematical and computational methods. No religious adjudication, assumed hidden messages, or presumption of ancient modern scientific knowledge. A null result is acceptable.

## First branch: OII-HERM-001
Begin with Poemandres, then the complete surviving Corpus Hermeticum, Asclepius, Stobaean fragments, Nag Hammadi Hermetica and the medieval Emerald Tablet (distinct textual history). Candidate source: https://gnosis.org/library/hermet.htm (Mead and other translations; edition and rights verification pending). Compare Greek and other original-language witnesses and modern scholarship where legally accessible. Hermes Trismegistus is an attributed literary figure, not an established historical author.

## Subsequent branches
Vedas and Upanishads; Egyptian and Mesopotamian texts; Plato and Aristotle; Chinese philosophical and scientific literature; Euclid and Archimedes; Aryabhata and Brahmagupta; Ibn al-Haytham and al-Khwarizmi; Leonardo, Galileo, Kepler and Newton. These are historical periods of different ages, not all ancient. Expand based on source availability and scholarly review.

## Method
1. Register edition, translator, date, passage identifier, manuscript provenance, rights and uncertainty. Preserve source separately from interpretation.
2. Conduct historical and linguistic readings, then independent computational, mathematical, philosophical and systems-engineering readings.
3. Use RABE-style recursive assumption review, alternative interpretations and adversarial evaluation. Preserve rejected branches.
4. Compare blinded and context-aware readings, using matched control passages and synthetic controls. Record potential author recognition, shared model biases and translation effects. Predefine scoring and criteria; independent sessions are not necessarily statistically independent.
5. Classify each claim as textual observation, historical interpretation, modern analogy, testable hypothesis or validated result. Formalize and test only where appropriate.
6. Publish methods, evidence, negative results, limitations and reproducible code where rights and safety permit. Invite criticism and relevant domain expertise.

## Initial hypotheses (unvalidated)
- Poemandres opening dialogue can be compared with interactive inquiry and source identification; this may be a generic dialogue feature.
- Archetypal and manifested worlds can be compared with abstract specifications and instantiated computational environments. This is a modern analogy, not historical evidence of computing.
- Test whether computational parallels occur more frequently or more specifically in Hermetic texts than matched controls. No superiority or novelty is assumed.

## Execution gates
Source inventory and rights review; pilot passage verification; preregistered control protocol; independent analyses; adversarial review; reproducible experiments if warranted; public report. Full corpus acquisition, worker execution, controls and experiments remain pending. Do not claim discoveries before validation.
