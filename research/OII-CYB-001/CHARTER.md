# OII-CYB-001 — Cybernetic Cognition and Closed-Loop Adaptive Systems

Authorized 2026-11-27. Status: proposed research; no qualification claimed. Civilian, independent, public-by-default subject to responsible release gates; no DARPA affiliation.

## Questions
1. Which historical cybernetic principles remain useful for modern integrated cognition? Review Wiener, feedback control, embodied cognition and prior cognitive architectures.
2. Does modular integration of observation, memory, executive control, prediction and feedback outperform a single-model baseline?
3. Can verified recurring behaviors be delegated to bounded low-level routines, reducing reasoning cost without increasing errors?
4. How do biological automaticity, proprioception, motor regulation and learning inform computational closed-loop control? Distinguish biological analogy from equivalence.
5. Can self-modeling, metacognition, uncertainty calibration and goal persistence be operationally tested? None establishes subjective consciousness.
6. Which organizational practices support long-horizon civilian inquiry, reproducibility and preservation without near-term commercialization?
7. What can existing brain-computer interfaces teach us about intention decoding, shared control, sensory feedback and restoration of autonomy?

## Tracks and gates
G00: Prior art, terminology, rights, evidence inventory and safety review. G01: Reproduce conventional feedback-controller baseline in simulation. G02: Compare flat versus hierarchical control under matched budgets. G03: Verified routine delegation with interruption, rollback and fail-closed tests. G04: Integrate synthetic proprioception and delayed/noisy feedback. G05: Compare Hydra placement and Reality OS world-state integration using separate tracks with bidirectional evidence exchange; no automatic promotion. G06: Evaluate uncertainty, self-model accuracy, persistence and recovery without consciousness claims. G07: Literature-only BCI review and simulated intention/feedback interface; compare read-only decoding, brain-spine bridging and direct sensory stimulation as distinct mechanisms. G08: Independent reproduction, civilian governance review, preservation and sanitized publication.

## Metrics
Task success, incorrect actions, latency distributions, compute, calibration, recovery, perturbation robustness, and reproducibility. Preregister thresholds against conventional controllers before experiments. Preserve negative outcomes and separate measured outcomes from forecasts.

## Boundaries
Simulation and public literature only. No implantation, human/animal experiments, neural stimulation instructions, personal neural data, clinical efficacy claims, autonomous weapon applications or unrestricted device control. Any future clinical work requires separate authorization, qualified partners and ethics/regulatory review. Human intent and override remain authoritative.
