# OII-TRM-001 — Fundamental Matter Manipulation and Computational Transmutation

**Designation:** Principal research program, alongside Reality OS and Hydra
**Established:** 2026-11-27
**Status:** Authorized exploratory research; no experimental results claimed
**Publication:** Public by default, subject to privacy, rights and safety review

## Mission
Investigate the extent to which physical matter can be treated as a programmable substrate, with particular emphasis on safe restoration of biological structure and function. Pursue computationally tractable questions even when physical validation is beyond available resources. Preserve reproducible results, failures and barriers for future investigators.

## Workstreams
- TRM: structural, molecular, atomic, nuclear and mass-energy transformations; conservation, thermodynamics, feasibility and limits.
- OII-BMR-001: Biological Matter Restoration, a linked workstream investigating tissue modeling, molecular repair, regenerative medicine and functional verification. Prioritize nonclinical simulation and established evidence; no promise of cures.
- Historical inquiry: compare primary alchemical sources, chronology, transmission and symbolism without assuming a lost technology.

## Research gates
1. Literature and provenance review; distinguish demonstrated capabilities from hypotheses.
2. Formal state and transformation model with conservation constraints and uncertainty.
3. Reproduce published benign benchmarks in chemistry and materials science.
4. Evaluate a simulated transformation planner against simple baselines; report energy, error and feasibility bounds.
5. Model nonclinical tissue restoration using synthetic or appropriately licensed data; evaluate functional constraints and uncertainty.
6. Independent review, reproducibility, negative results and barrier register.
7. Publish cleared evidence and next questions; physical or clinical work requires separate qualified oversight and approvals.

## Portfolio integration
Hydra may support bounded planning and independent verification, subject to benchmark evidence. Reality OS may visualize models and uncertainty without implying physical validation. Neither integration is assumed beneficial until tested.

## Governance and limits
No creation from literal nothingness is assumed. No universal matter manipulator has been demonstrated. Nuclear or mass-energy control does not imply biological precision. No human experimentation, clinical recommendations, hazardous physical procedures or sensitive personal data are authorized by this charter. Restrict dangerous operational details. Maintain human oversight, independent scrutiny and open, decipherable records. No investigator is expected to sacrifice personal wellbeing. Stop at documented computational, physical, ethical or resource limits.

## Continuity
Revisit barriers when new evidence, methods or resources become available. A future solution is a possibility, not a prediction. The program is intended to remain useful beyond its founders.

*Tomorrow’s technology. Today’s computation.*
