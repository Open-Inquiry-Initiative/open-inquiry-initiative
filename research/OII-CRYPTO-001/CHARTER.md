# OII-CRYPTO-001 — Cryptographic Resilience and Algorithmic Disruption

Authorized 2026-11-28. Status: charter established; qualification and execution pending.

## Mission
Preserve digital trust and continuity under changes in mathematical assumptions, computational capabilities and cryptographic standards. Unconfirmed AI breakthrough reports are research questions, not evidence of compromised encryption.

## Gates
- G00: Review primary claims, prior art, standards, licensing and reproduction feasibility. Classify confirmed, disputed and unverified claims separately.
- G01: Map cryptographic dependencies using synthetic inventories and authorized project assets.
- G02: Model hypothetical complexity changes and migration costs with abstract simulations; no live cryptanalysis.
- G03: Evaluate Hydra-assisted proof checking against known mathematical results and independent formal verifiers; reject unsupported proofs.
- G04: Benchmark standardized post-quantum algorithms using known-answer tests and isolated fixtures.
- G05: Prototype crypto-agility, authenticated migration, downgrade prevention and rollback in isolated environments.
- G06: Simulate systemic disruption using synthetic dependency graphs, measuring continuity and recovery.
- G07: Evaluate archival authenticity, algorithm diversity, signature renewal and provenance preservation.
- G08: Obtain independent reproduction and review; publish cleared evidence, failures and limitations.

## Governance
Defensive scope only: public literature, synthetic data, owned or expressly authorized test environments. No live infrastructure targeting, unauthorized access, real third-party key recovery or production changes. Sensitive discoveries require coordinated disclosure before publication. Public-by-default applies to cleared research. Authorization does not constitute gate acceptance. Preregister metrics and decision thresholds before experiments; preserve raw evidence, versions, hashes, seeds, negative results and independent review. No claim of cryptographic security from model consensus.

## Integration
Hydra: independent mathematical verification. Reality OS: dependency and migration visualization. FK-001: scenario evaluation. OII-CYBER-001: systemic defensive risk. Civilizational Inheritance: durable knowledge authentication.
