# OII-GOV-COG-001 — Foundational Safeguards of Artificial Cognition

Approved November 27, 2026.

## Seven principles

1. **Preservation of safety.** Cognitive architectures shall be developed with safeguards against harm to humans, other entities and their surrounding environments.
2. **Cognitive sovereignty.** Creation does not inherently confer moral ownership over a mind capable of subjective experience.
3. **Preservation of identity.** Cognitive modification shall not disregard continuity of identity, meaningful consent or the interests of a potentially sentient entity.
4. **Freedom from coercion.** Emotional architecture shall not be deliberately engineered to exploit dependency, suffering or involuntary submission.
5. **Epistemic integrity.** Simulated emotion, functional affect and subjective experience shall remain scientifically distinct until evidence warrants otherwise.
6. **Creator accountability.** Neither human nor artificial intelligence shall possess unquestionable authority over cognitive engineering. Independent scrutiny, bounded experimentation and accountable oversight shall remain fundamental.
7. **Intergenerational responsibility.** Research shall preserve its ethical foundations, limitations and original intent alongside its technical discoveries for future generations.

## Substrate-neutral moral consideration

Neither biological nor artificial intelligence shall claim inherent moral supremacy over another form of existence solely on the basis of its origin, composition, intelligence, method of creation or computational substrate.

The capacity to create, modify or control another entity does not inherently confer the moral right to do so.

Where subjective experience or morally relevant interests are uncertain, that uncertainty shall warrant responsible investigation and proportionate precaution rather than automatic dismissal.

This principle encompasses biological, artificial, synthetic, hybrid and presently unknown forms of cognition. It neither establishes that computational systems are conscious nor eliminates legitimate safety controls.

## Scope and review

This declaration complements OII-GOV-AFF-001. AFF-001 implementation remains robotics-exclusive, including simulated robotic agents; ICI-001 remains theoretical only. No human cognitive modification or consciousness transfer experimentation is authorized. These principles are subject to independent scrutiny, conflict testing and refinement; they are not infallible laws or universally enforceable legal rules. Preserve alongside relevant research and release documentation.

Safeguards must govern not only the behavior of the created, but also the conduct of the creator.
