# OPEN INQUIRY INITIATIVE
### Tomorrow's technology. Today's computation.

OII-MANIFESTO-001 · Adopted November 27, 2026

We are not content to sit and wait for the future to arrive.

We investigate the technologies of tomorrow using the computational capabilities of today.

Where hardware has yet to mature, we explore the software foundations. Where engineering has yet to catch up with imagination, we construct models, develop prototypes, test hypotheses and preserve our discoveries.

We distinguish possibility from proof, speculation from demonstrated capability, and ambition from scientific evidence.

Our purpose is not to monopolize the future, but to make its foundations accessible to those who will inherit it.

**We are not waiting for tomorrow. We are building toward it, so that when tomorrow arrives, we are already there.**
